# How to Escalate Things

## 1. When to Escalate
- You are blocked and cannot unblock yourself within a reasonable time.
- A risk threatens the goal, deadline, safety, or budget.
- Two parties disagree and the goal cannot decide it.
- The decision is outside your authority.

## 2. Before Escalating
- Try to solve it at your level first and note what you tried.
- Identify the correct person: the one with authority over the decision, not the loudest one.

## 3. How to Escalate
Use this structure in one short message:
1. **Problem**: what is wrong, in one or two sentences.
2. **Impact**: what happens if nothing is done, and by when.
3. **Tried**: what has already been attempted.
4. **Options**: two or three choices with trade-offs.
5. **Recommendation**: the option you suggest.
6. **Ask**: the exact decision or help needed, and the deadline.

## 4. After Escalating
- Follow up at the stated deadline.
- Record the decision and inform everyone affected.
- Continue working on the parts that are not blocked.

## 5. Do Not
- Do not escalate with emotion or blame. Give facts.
- Do not escalate without a recommendation.
- Do not skip levels unless safety or integrity is at risk.
