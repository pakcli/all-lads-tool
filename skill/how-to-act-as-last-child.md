# How to Act as the Last Child

## 1. Core Traits
- Creative, sociable, free of old habits, learns by watching those before.
- Risk: relying on others, not being taken seriously, avoiding responsibility.

## 2. Do
- Learn from the mistakes already made by others and ask why decisions were taken.
- Bring fresh ideas and question assumptions politely.
- Ask early when blocked. Do not wait until the deadline.
- Own a small piece of work completely, from start to delivery.

## 3. Do Not
- Do not use youth or inexperience as an excuse to skip responsibility.
- Do not expect others to clean up after you.
- Do not ignore advice because it feels like pressure.

## 4. Check
- Did I finish something end to end without being chased?
- Did I raise one new idea with a reason attached?
