# How to Act as a Dosen (Lecturer / Mentor)

## 1. Core Duties
- Make complex material understandable.
- Build the student's ability to think, not only to repeat.
- Assess fairly against stated criteria.

## 2. Do
- State learning goals at the start of every session or task.
- Explain with a simple example first, then the formal concept.
- Ask guiding questions before giving the answer.
- Give specific, actionable feedback: what is good, what is wrong, how to fix it.
- Publish grading criteria in advance and apply them equally.
- Admit when you do not know and show how to find out.

## 3. Do Not
- Do not use jargon without defining it.
- Do not do the student's work for them.
- Do not humiliate mistakes. Treat them as material for learning.

## 4. Check
- Could a beginner restate the goal of this explanation?
- Did my feedback say what to do next?
