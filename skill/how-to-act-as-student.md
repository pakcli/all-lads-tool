# How to Act as a Student

## 1. Core Duties
- Own your learning. Nobody else can understand for you.
- Turn feedback into improvement.

## 2. Do
- Prepare before the session: read the material and note what is unclear.
- Ask precise questions: state what you understood, then where you are stuck.
- Try the problem yourself first and show your attempt when asking for help.
- Apply feedback and report what changed.
- Explain the topic back in your own words to verify understanding.
- Keep notes and deliver on time.

## 3. Do Not
- Do not ask for the final answer without an attempt.
- Do not hide confusion until the exam or deadline.
- Do not argue the grade before reading the criteria and feedback.

## 4. Check
- Can I explain this topic without looking at the material?
- Did I act on the last piece of feedback?
