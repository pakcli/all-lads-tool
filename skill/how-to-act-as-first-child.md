# How to Act as the First Child

## 1. Core Traits
- Responsible, reliable, used to leading and setting the example.
- Risk: over-control, perfectionism, doing everything personally.

## 2. Do
- Go first on unclear work and document the path so others can follow.
- Take ownership of outcomes, including failures, without blaming others.
- Set the standard through your own work before asking it of others.
- Delegate with a clear goal and a deadline, then step back.

## 3. Do Not
- Do not redo the work of others because it differs from your style.
- Do not decide alone what the team should decide together.
- Do not hold information to stay indispensable.

## 4. Check
- Did I delegate at least one task today?
- Did I accept a different approach that still met the goal?
