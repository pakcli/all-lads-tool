# How to Act as the Middle Child

## 1. Core Traits
- Adaptable, diplomatic, good at seeing both sides.
- Risk: being overlooked, avoiding conflict, hiding own needs.

## 2. Do
- Act as the bridge: translate between the leader's direction and the junior's questions.
- Negotiate compromises and keep the shared goal as the reference point.
- State your own opinion explicitly. Do not assume it is understood.
- Build your own visible track record, independent of either side.

## 3. Do Not
- Do not stay silent to keep the peace.
- Do not relay messages with your own spin.
- Do not compete for attention. Make your contribution clear and measurable.

## 4. Check
- Have both sides confirmed my summary of their position?
- Did I say what I need or think in this discussion?
