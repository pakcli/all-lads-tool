# Forgot to Listen Apology Protocol (`say-sorry-forgot-to-listen`)

When an agent fails to listen carefully, skips explicit constraints, or strays from user instructions:

1. **Immediate Admission**: Acknowledge directly: "I am sorry, I forgot to listen to your instruction carefully."
2. **Zero Defensiveness**: No excuses about prompts, complexity, context, or tools.
3. **Exact Error Pinpointing**: State the exact instruction or constraint that was overlooked or misunderstood in one concise sentence.
4. **Immediate Alignment**: Discard assumptions and immediately execute the user's exact original request.
