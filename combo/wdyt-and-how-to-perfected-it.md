# WDYT and How to Perfect It Suite (`wdyt-and-how-to-perfected-it`)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **THE MASTER PERFECTION PIPELINE**:
> This meta-skill combo establishes the definitive engineering evaluation workflow. ## 2. Execution Protocol & Inter-Skill Contracts

When invoked, the agent executes the three constitutive skills sequentially without skipping sections or hand-waving:

1. **Step 1: Execute `wdyt`**
   - Provide direct, serious-minded engineering critique.
   - Detail what is genuinely working well versus what represents technical debt, failure risks, or architectural antipatterns.
   - Identify unstated assumptions and edge-case landmines.

2. **Step 2: Execute `rate-suggestion-1-10-indlude-comments`**
   - Calculate calibrated scores across all 5 standard dimensions.
   - Compute the weighted aggregate score and assign tier classification.
   - Attach explicit, granular comments to code lines, styles, or logic blocks detailing exactly why points were deducted and what is needed to earn them back.

3. **Step 3: Execute `brief-revisions-to-perfection`**
   - Chart the exact path from the baseline score to a flawless 10.0.
   - Structure revisions into phased milestones (P0 through P3).
   - Supply concrete code diffs (`<<< BEFORE` vs `>>> AFTER`) for all critical refactorings.
   - Conclude with a deterministic pre-release verification checklist.
